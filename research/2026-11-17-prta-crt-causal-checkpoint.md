## Primitive Rotating Triangular Algebra (PRTA) and rhombus transport

**Source:** Joseph Fredrickson, *Source-Owned Contact Release and Collision-Free Boundary Detour*, PRTA–rhombus v0.38 (17 November 2026), supplied four-page manuscript and companion archive.

- The depth-33 fixture contains **1,679** existing, named triangular faces. The depth is supplied; it is **not** recovered from a semiprime.
- Under an explicitly specified release of the original B–slot13 contact, retention of B–slot11, fixed other faces, and unchanged orientation, the UP source has a **unique** receiving port at U(6,−40).
- Under the separately verified v0.37 component-frontier condition, the DOWN source has a **unique** companion placement at D(6,−40).
- An exact rational separating-axis audit certified a **seven-segment**, two-source planar detour without positive-area penetration of stationary triangular interiors; temporary release of whole-edge contact is allowed.
- A general no-motion argument applies when anchored, fixed-orientation congruent triangles must remain continuously connected through complete whole-edge contacts at every instant.
- **Not established:** a spontaneous initiating contact release from the unchanged state, all-instant legal movement under the stronger original contact grammar, or a general factor-recovery method.

The LRD and broader triangular-algebra investigations are separate, active research threads; the v0.38 results should not be attributed to all triangular algebras.

## Constructive Runtime — authenticated incremental proof revalidation

**Source:** Joseph Fredrickson, *Constructive Runtime v1.4.5 — Authenticated Incremental Proof Revalidation* (17 November 2026), 11-page supplied technical record.

- Native **C++17** indexed witness store, SHA-256 Merkle commitments, transition receipts, and an independent **Python** receipt verifier.
- Controlled synthetic fixture: **2,048 records**, **131,072 separately retained source occurrences**, seven trials at each of six edit fractions on Windows and hosted Linux.
- At **1%** changed records, sparse receipt production plus independent check took median **13.076 ms** on Windows and **3.930 ms** on Linux; fresh certificate production took **35.223 ms** and **32.850 ms**, respectively.
- **14/14** dedicated Windows checks passed. Negative tests explicitly demonstrate that a poisoned untouched witness or an unauthorized external file mutation cannot be ruled out by a sparse receipt alone.
- **Important:** sparse and fresh timings have different trust/verification scopes; they exclude full-source materialization, complete legacy audit, and Clang compilation. **No end-to-end incremental compilation speedup is established.**
- The report proposes direct authenticated compiler-gate consumption as a **future experiment**, not an implemented v1.4.5 result.

This compiler-proof line is distinct from the earlier C++20 HTTP downloader/cache v0.23 line; their version numbers are not interchangeable.

## Output-only causal influence and witnessed nonconstruction

**Source:** *Output-Only Forward Influence and Witnessed Nonconstruction*, native SHA-256 / elliptic-curve intervention audit v6 (November 2026), supplied research record.

- Two independently implemented learners used observed output changes to predict affected-output sets in a fixed intervention contract.
- The reported **192 held-out intervention cases** matched affected-output footprints; the report also preserves a counterexample showing that positive observations alone cannot identify responses to withheld required constructions.
- **Not established:** universal causal identification, hidden-operation reconstruction, or cryptographic inversion.

## Research reporting policy

Every result should identify its input contract, tested fixture, independent checker, counterexamples, and open limitations. A conditional theorem is not an unconditional physical mechanism; a recorded test is not external replication; and an authenticated proof update is not yet an incremental compiler.
